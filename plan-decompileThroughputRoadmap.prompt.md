## Plan: Decompile Throughput Roadmap

Prioritize the next 10 big areas around evidence yield, not broad gameplay completion. The fastest path forward is to tighten the decompile/export loop, standardize spell and packet evidence capture, then attack the specific client/server boundaries that are still blocking large chunks of mapping: spell result/broadcast messages, cast-context and service-token flow, prerequisite checks, RavelSignal consumers, CSI callback tables, and NavMap/LoS. The target-helper chain is now a completed foundation area rather than the lead blocker. Implementation under this plan means durable labels, documentation, packet/model cleanup, diagnostics, and only conservative server mutations once a boundary reaches Verified evidence.
