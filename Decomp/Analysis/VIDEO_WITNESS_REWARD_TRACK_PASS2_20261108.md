- Correlated, track 16 (type 3, thresholds 100/200/300, name 700181 "Reward Track - Introductory Zones (Level 3-6)"): RewardTrackRewards 320 (one choice, item 12045 Small Bag), 321 (12045 / 81917 VendiShot Mk. This is exactly what C shows, in the same order, and the cumulative "N more points" labels match thresholds 100/200/300.
- Correlated, track 35: the item is 86274 Icebound Pouch and the currency row is type 1 / 250000 (row 535). The 2016 PTR values are a factor 10 below the 16042 tables twice: threshold 600000 against 6000000, and the claim's 2 Platinum 50 Gold (2,500,000 copper) against 250000. Two independent ratios of 10 point to a preview-to-final rescale rather than a test character. This does not make the final rows wrong; the 16042 table values stay authoritative for 16042, but the 2016 clips are not magnitudes for it. Tooltip text also differs ("for a chance to obtain one of" versus table text "Open to obtain one of"), another sign the preview text changed.
- Correlated, text: 745159 is the panel description, 745296 "Reward is ready to claim!" and 678885/678886/678888 are "Reward $1c (already claimed) / (ready to claim) / ($2n more points)", the three labels seen in C.
- Correlated, achievement: 683000 "On the Right Track" is Achievement 6095, counted at 1 completion; 6096 and 6097 count 5 and 10 completions ("Complete the Arcterra Reward Track one/five/ten times", ids 683001..683008). This agrees with A, where the banner appears right after the first claim, and says completions are counted and repeatable.
- Lua (`I:\tools\extract_lua\UI`): ChallengeLog.lua 864 and Contracts.lua 1180 call `ClaimRewardPoint(nRewardIdx, nChoiceIdx)` from a button, so claim is a client-initiated request; Challenge Log shows "Claim Reward!" only when `bCanClaim and not bIsClaimed` and disables it until a choice is picked. ZoneMap.lua 1495 to 1561 shows the Zone track as one reward with one choice, label from `GetRewardPointsEarned()` over `arRewards[1].nCost`, percent `progress / cost * 100` unclamped (matches 156% and 170%, floored), crown button enabled by `bCanClaim`, and a ClaimPrompt popup when the map is not open (matches the toast in B). The reset is not in Lua: after the click the engine reports the lower counter.

## Effect on the 07CE / track gates (Observed, not implemented)
1. Claim is player-initiated (click on the crown or Claim button), not automatic at the threshold. Points keep accruing above 100% until claimed; the reward is delivered as loot messages (item plus currency) at click time.
2. Claim reduced the counter by exactly the threshold (600000) in two independent clips (937519 to 337519, 1022313 to 422313): excess carries over, it is not discarded or zeroed. "Reset upon reaching 100%" therefore reads as "subtract one threshold when claimed", at the claim click, not at the moment 100% is crossed.
3. A ready-to-claim notification (popup when the map is closed, glowing crown, tooltip) is fired when the track update marks `bCanClaim`.
4. Per-activity gains seen: challenge bronze 100 on the intro track (Signature 125); contract tiers 50/150/450 (speech). Arcterra per-activity amounts are not visible: the counters were only seen before and after long stretches of play. Public-event completion coincided with the claim toast in B but the points it added are not shown.
5. Public 07CE stays unwired. The record narrows the missing witness; it does not by itself change server behaviour.
