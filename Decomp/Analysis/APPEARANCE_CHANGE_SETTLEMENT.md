## Settlement and publication

The existing frozen Character fee actor owns the accepted operation, its
normal Auth save, complete Character enrollment, retry and ordinary followup.
A distinct `AppearanceChangeSaveReconciliation` checks the locked SQL payer,
original appearance slice, route and complete target. The target replaces the
whole slice, including removed rows; it is restaged through followup without
publishing provisional Player fields. Direct manager, header and retained leaf
mutations are fenced while the operation owns that slice. Signed zero is
canonicalized because providers may normalize its floating-point representation.

Credits join the complete Character transaction through the existing frozen
wallet. Nonzero Service Token fees use a narrowly typed appearance descriptor
and sealed Character receipt in the existing durable account-payment protocol.
There is no vendor-item, guild or residence authority in this receipt. A rejected
Character graph compensates the durable Auth debit before the same frozen actor
may retry. Lost commit replies are resolved against the complete receipt and
durable account operation rather than charging again. New admission recognizes
a paid decision or finishes a refund from its retained debit anchor. Free fees
create no account-payment anchor. No schema migration is required.

Only confirmed work is adopted by the original actor. Fresh bone packet
factories and a retained cursor resume failed sends without replaying payment
or successful notices. Source and visible-recipient identities, sessions,
queues, maps and epochs are checked again at publication. A replacement
session receives no old success. Changing a retained owner after capture keeps
unresolved work fenced; this does not establish deployed cleanup/restart timing.

Pricing and projection operate on frozen request values. Their callbacks
cannot redirect an incoming request to another Player, the same Player on a
replacement session, or replacement wallets. Existing catalog validation and
prices remain authoritative; this change invents no retail price, bone range
or refund policy. Structural admission remains described in
[the earlier result](APPEARANCE_CHANGE_ADMISSION.md).
