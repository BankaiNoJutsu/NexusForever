## Ownership and presentation

`CharacterSelectHandler` retains the selected Player, Account and map position. `ChallengeManager.PrepareForWorld` loads the durable reward snapshot through the existing executor and actor event queue. Its readiness callback waits for all queued reward ownership to end, including work queued after the load, so map admission cannot run while the old SQL/inventory lease is still held. The callback is bound to the original actor character, Session, Account and Account.Session; disconnect, logout, invalidation, replacement and authoritative-reload requirements prevent admission. The handler separately checks its captured session Player/Account before adding to the map. A callback failure disconnects instead of leaving a ready pregame session hanging.

Character reward data is expected during startup, but its contribution is not published ahead of bootstrap. The first `SendInitialPackets` publishes the already-loaded snapshot. Repeated calls retain the existing single-reload behavior. Later committed mutations continue to publish their durable snapshots. Choose/claim remains unauthorized until the composed full reload actually includes that exact Character snapshot. Loading does not create a track, cycle, medal, claim, item or attempt.

Production managers reject Challenge choices, shares and progress and pause Challenge timers until the initial load is ready. Real selection keeps them outside the map during that interval. Constructor/custom managers without production reward initialization preserve their existing behavior. The normal-save snapshots from checkpoint 7870 can now use the cached database identity from the start of actual world gameplay.
