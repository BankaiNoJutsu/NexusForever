## Failure behaviour
- Money short: no footage. Client Lua (`I:\tools\extract_lua\UI\TaxiMap\TaxiMap.lua`, lines 872-880 and 626-627): `bCantAfford = nPlayerAmount < nPrice` only tints the price red in the hover tooltip; `OnTaxiMapButtonDown` still calls `PurchaseFlightPath` (no money test, no confirm dialog, no message). The only click messages are `TaxiMap_CantRoute` and `TaxiMap_NotUnlocked`, a 4 s banner. So the rejection message, if any, is server-driven. Text table has "Insufficient Funds" (id 178065), "Not enough Money" (119716-119719), "Not enough cash" (73752); `FloatText.lua` maps `Vendor_NotEnoughCash` to "" (no floating text) so any visible message would be a chat/system line, not a float. Which string a failed taxi click produces: unknown.
- Combat: `HandleDisabledBlocker` (lines 344-367) puts a "RapidTransport_Combat" blocker over the **taxi window itself when the player is in combat** (also in tutorial zones); so combat likely stops the window rather than a ride. Unwitnessed in footage.
- Text ids show retail rules exist for actions on a taxi: 73922 "You can't do that while on a taxi", 119741 "You are on a taxi", 27953 "You Cannot Cast While on a Taxi", 158144 "Taxi conditions not met", 73807 "No Spline for Taxi". No footage of any of them.
- Second taxi while one is active, death or dismount in flight, disconnect: **no footage found**. In W4 the second party member's taxi flies in parallel, each charged independently (only the recording player's wallet is visible).
- Free-flight HUD note: W1 hides the HUD during the ride (player choice or taxi mode), FloatText skips zone-change text while `GetPlayerTaxiUnit()` is set (FloatText.lua 158-160).

## Conclusions (Observed tier)
1. Single-hop fare: debited when the taxi vehicle reaches the pad (0-0.8 s after the map closes in three clips, 4 s in W4 where the vehicle was late), before takeoff; the wallet never changes again at landing. Four clips, four exact tier fares (132, 531, 132, and 531 first leg).
2. Multi-hop fare: charged in legs during the flight (W4), so moving the whole debit to the purchase handler would be a guess unsupported by footage; "debit at destination admission" (today) matches none of the clips (the arrival debit would be the last leg only).
3. Failure/interrupt semantics: no retail footage; only the client Lua facts above.
