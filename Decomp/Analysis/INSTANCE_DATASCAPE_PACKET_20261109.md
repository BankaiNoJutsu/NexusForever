## 0. Scope, method and honesty notes
* **Corpus size.** 99 dated retail clips are zone-tagged Datascape (73 with subtitles, 2014-06-27 to 2017-11-17, none before the 2014-06-02 launch); 116 match by title or description (86 with subtitles). The first retail kills are 2014-08-18 (System Daemons). The 2014-06-27 clip https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWGN-gIk8cY ("Intro/Big Robots", no subtitles) predates the instance's release, so it is an early/PTR clip, not used.
* **Scale.** Datascape shipped as a **40-man** raid (every Zybak "40 Man" and spythe "(40)" clip, August 2014 to about April 2015) and a **20-man** version appears from February 2015 (spythe "(20)" clips on 2015-02-13, Afterlife 2015-02-20 onward, moepork 2015-12). Mechanics read are the same bosses at both sizes; whether the `Creature2` rows differ per size is **unknown** (second rows exist: Avatus 60057 and 60058 beside the repo's 30505, Mnemesis 60068 beside 30504, both with the same difficulty id as the base row). Normal versus veteran does not apply as far as read; the `Immortal ...` objectives are optional challenges. Each clip below is tagged with its size where the title says so.
* **What the subtitles are, and the main weakness of this raid.** Almost every Datascape clip is a raid-voice POV (spythe, Zybak minibosses, Nanomancy/Cymatics, NorZZa, Alepap): the captions are callouts, not explanation. A keyword-density scan of the zone-tagged subtitled clips under 2000 s plus title searches and a spot read of the top hits found **two narrated boss sources only: moepork's System Daemons guide and Zybak's System Daemons strategy video**, plus warrior-build guides with no boss content (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sPJGXic_rrA). The scan is a heuristic and could miss a narrated clip; clips over 2000 s were not scanned. For every other boss this packet has **vocabulary and numbers from comms, not a skeleton**; that is stated per boss. Names are mangled by auto-captions.
* **Not done:** no timing measurement, no cast-bar reading, no unsubtitled clip read (Shyn To Krautzwerk, BusyBard Nostalgia, most spythe 2014 "(40)" pulls without subs, and the 3 to 4 hour Kaos Sverige raid nights were not read), no native read.
* **Table facts used (Mapped to objective and creature ids):** `PublicEventObjective` rows for event 157 (read by column position, matching the repo `PublicEventObjectiveEntry` order and the numbers in the texts), `Creature2` names through the text table, and Spell4 rows. Challenge texts are quoted per boss.
* **Id-block note:** ability rows do not cluster per boss (see the Kel Voreth correction); the System Daemons names cluster in 43002 to 43478, the Elemental Guardian internal tags in 70456 to 79420, but boss bindings below rest on names plus tags or anchors, never id adjacency.
