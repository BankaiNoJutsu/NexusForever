* **Text anchors:** Spell4 36324 -> text 292911 "Thunderfoot's Rage increases all Damage Dealt..."; Spell4 81706 -> texts 690850 and 690851 "Katja Zarkhov has turned ravenous...".

## Decision on the native read
A native read of the client cannot supply cast order or intervals: boss AI runs on the server and the client only holds the spell data already in the tables. The creature-to-spell mapping and timing would need a server-side source (packet capture or the original scripts), neither of which is in this environment. Cast order and intervals therefore remain Observed-from-video only, and per the prompt no boss slice that needs them is implemented. The earlier suggestion to commission a native read for this purpose is withdrawn.

## Mechanical id/name check of the packets (2026-11-09, lead ran it)
A script extracted every `NNNN "Name"` pair (four to six digit number followed by a quoted name) from the 13 packet files and compared it with the Spell4 name and description-tag text when the number is a Spell4 id: 98 pairs, 63 match, 35 do not. Reading the 35: they are overwhelmingly numbers that are not spell claims (objective ids such as 4554/4556/4558 and 1880, creature ids such as 23455 "Ransacker", map ids such as 3041, text fragments the pattern caught such as "and" or sentence pieces) that happen to fall inside the Spell4 id range. I did not triage every one individually and I did not check pairs written in other formats (tables, prose), so this is a partial check, not proof; no wrong spell claim was identified among the 35. Packets with the most Spell4 claims that matched: Sanctuary 12 of 12, Skullcano 11 of 11, Stormtalon bosses 2 and 3 15 of 17.

## Closure statement against the prompt's stop condition (2026-11-09)
The condition is that every instance row is either implemented to its evidence tier or has an exact statement of the missing input. Status by row group:
* **Implemented to tier:** none beyond kill credit and objective routing that existed before, plus two disabled phase placeholders (Blade-Wind 60%, single-source and disputed; Trogun 50%, four-clip) with no gameplay effect. No boss casts, phases or adds are implemented.
* **Exact missing input (stated in each packet's open inputs, agent-written, partly spot-checked here):** cast order and intervals for every boss (no table or client source; server-side), the creature-to-spell and tier-per-version mapping, health thresholds that two independent clips agree on (most are single-source or disputed), positions, spawn counts and timings; plus per-row gaps: Ultimate Protogames world 3041 and the 3524 and 3102 raid worlds have no footage, BAD (3042) has no event, map entry or script, Coldblood Citadel has one narrated clip without numbers, Datascape and Genetic Archives sub-bosses without usable footage, Rage Logic four clips from one commentator.
* **Not met:** lead verification of the agent-written packets is partial (tag join, two text anchors, the id check above); the Phase A engine items (interrupt effect on a pending scripted cast, invulnerable adds, wipe reset, wiring the tracker into scripts) remain open.
The honest reading is that the "missing input" half of the condition is documented for every row and the "implemented" half is not met, because the inputs that would let a boss be implemented (cast order and intervals) are not available in this environment.

## Lead check: do the scripted abilities appear in retail subtitles? (2026-11-09)
Script `scripts/instance_verify_abilities.py`, output `coverage/provisional_ability_subtitle_check_20261109.csv`. For each of the 48 Spell4 rows used by the provisional scripts it takes the ability name from the row's description tag, then counts retail-dated (non-empty upload date before 2018-11-28), subtitled videos in the matching dungeon (video title, description or zone mentioning the dungeon or one of its bosses) whose subtitles contain that phrase (also the joined spelling), and the distinct channels among them.
Result: **16 of 48 rows have the exact ability name in subtitles from two or more channels, 11 from one channel, 21 from none.** Examples with two or more channels: Volcanic Strike (5), Thrash (9), Lightning Strike (6), Static Charge (3), Lightning Rod (2), Purge (10), Outbreak (4), Terraformer (7), Ink (6), Chromosome Corruption (2), Pulverize (2), Molten Rain (2), Shade Prison (2), Memory Wipe (3). With none: Thunder Cross spelled apart finds only one video, Torrent one, Forgemaster's Call, Exanite Weapon, Headbutt, Magma Totem Pair, Harpoon, Disperse Grenades, Holo Hands, Holo Cannons, Strain Bomb (one), DNA Siphon, Detonation Bombs, Aerial Bombardment, Seeking Shadow, Soulfrost Trail, Howling Winds, Flame Leash, Smoldering Pool, Shadow Meld, Lights Out, Summon the Swarm.
How to read it: this is an exact-name search over auto-generated subtitles, so it undercounts (commentators use their own names, "hook shot" for Harpoon, "Forge Master's Call", "lightning cross"; a few keywords are weak, for example `Boss` for Wrathbone and `PBAE Root 1` for Ondu). A hit means the name is said in the dungeon's videos, not that the boss casts it or that the timing is right. A zero means the name was not confirmed this way, not that it is wrong. The 21 zero rows rest on the agent packets alone and are the first to review; all provisional scripts stay disabled by default.
