payment bound to the complete Character/guild/residence creation decision.

## Completed integrated behavior

- Capture GameFormula1159's accepted alternate fee and the admitted account,
  Character, realm, epoch and database routes. The real public handler uses the
  existing private Community draft and full graph coordinator; Character
  credits are not charged for a token request. A zero alternate fee retains
  free creation without an Auth debit.
- A distinct token creation receipt owns the exact Community/ranks/leader/
  affiliation/root/plots graph. It also identifies the payer, fee and original
  Auth route. Actor enrollment stays synchronous on the original World queue.
  Only after that complete Character graph is sealed, while its serializable
  owner lock remains held, may Auth authorize the payment. The SQL Character
  must still belong to the admitted payer.
- Reuse the existing durable account-payment/recovery protocol with an explicit
  Community descriptor and `community-create-debit:`/refund keys. It uses no
  fake item or CREDD usage intent. The Auth anchor binds the sealed token
  receipt hash, its Character database fingerprint/version and exact parents.
  An ordinary Guild or credit-only Community receipt cannot authorize a token
  debit. Generic vendor enrollment also rejects this distinct delivery type.
  Historical vendor/CREDD payloads omit the new null descriptor and retain their
  existing identity and recovery rules. No schema/backfill change was needed.
- Auth confirms its idempotent debit before Character flush. Lost replies from
  either database are observed through fresh receipts. A proven Character
  rollback first writes the durable cancellation decision and compensates its
  Auth payment before releasing the original retry owners. An unresolved refund
  retains that task and original ownership. A retry cannot accumulate another
  net debit for the accepted creation.
- Realm admission scans the durable Community payment anchors through the
  existing recovery barrier. A matching complete sealed Character receipt
  proves delivery; an absent decision becomes a durable cancellation and refund.
  Fresh recovery owners can finish an interrupted compensation, and neither a
  wrong database nor a foreign Character owner may decide that payment.
- Reserve the original token wallet after the earlier normal Auth save. After
  primary Character confirmation, read it asynchronously and adopt it on the
  original World queue before ordinary followup. Failed reads retain their
  reservation and can retry. Guild/residence registry adoption and success wait
  for followup. Independent wallet notices have retained cursors and fresh packet
  constructors, so a rejected later notice does not replay an accepted grant or
  payment. A replacement Player receives neither the wallet nor creation success.

The existing ordinary Guild and Community credit/free/admin routes remain
covered. Defaults15:1/17:1, no automatic44/seven grants, and the native client/
