## 2. Skeleton (Observed; "A" = two independent sources, "S" = single-source)
**Dungeon structure (A: Gamescom design talk, SywoGG guide):** random sequence of event rooms plus bosses; SywoGG: the first rooms vary but "the last one and the before-last one" are always the same; Gamescom: "six event rooms and three bosses", order and selection random per run; rewards come in periodic medals based on points; optional objectives add points and challenges multiply them (A); room entry via the safe zone/blue ring and exit teleport. The repo already handles this scaffold (Start Button 3250, "Go to the next event" objectives 3271 to 3275).

**Rooms/bosses the clips describe (names are from the dev talk/client text; the repo enum uses the same objective names):**
* Bev-O-Rage (Protostar break room, objective 2680, always the second-to-last room, "round number four ... Pug breaker" in SywoGG): you click the vending machine boss, are knocked down, then a gauntlet of laser mines, flame bursts and rotating beams in a corridor; the boss has interruptible casts that must be interrupted to start the next event ("the next event" is a bomb you pick up: DPS bomb with a sword, interrupt bomb, healing bomb with a 30 s cast and 100k heal check); each event ends with a pull back into the corridor; a barrel rolls down the hallway behind you (one-hit kill, jump pad to hop it); a challenge to take no telegraph in the hallway and another to collect coins; SywoGG mentions an interrupt-count challenge (about 36, garbled). Gamescom: the barrel is rolled "Indiana Jones style", kills if it hits, you run back to the start and return. A for structure (barrel, gauntlet, bomb-driven phases); the numbers (36 interrupts, 100k heal, 30 s) are single-source.
* Hut-Hut (Intern disposal facility, objective 2675, always last): American-football pastiche; the Protostar intern is the ball; the group must carry/pass/throw it into the end zone; two small adds buff the boss and must be separated from him; breaking his yellow shield makes him fumble and each goal gives a ~10 s moment of opportunity (SywoGG); a bonus "extra point" round uses a line of small mobs where the smallest ("kicker") must be passed with the ball; a thrower cannot immediately re-pick the intern; stay beside, not in front, of Hut-Hut when he has the ball and shield (SywoGG, S). Gamescom confirms: score more goals than the boss, shield break, fumble, passing, extra-point wave of mini Hut-Huts, "Hut-Hut-Hike" (A).
* The hungry boss (a fan-art creature whose design came from a player's child, per Gamescom): fed food it grows hungry/"hangry", attacks get harder, challenges to feed it specific food or none, bombs to feed it that explode it (Gamescom, S); SywoGG describes a boss that "eats" a player in an interruptible 4-stack cast (three armor plus one; "2 out of 10 stacks" challenge counter, up to three stacks if interrupted at 0.5 s remaining; devouring does not kill the player unless the cast completes) (S). Client names: room "Slimy Feast" (text 577336-577337), creature "Slimy Feast" Creature2 61468 (text 577615), buff group text 580448 "[UP] Slimy Feast - Buffs" (TargetGroup 10199), and Spell4 71130 "Slimy Feast Growth" (name-bound, Correlated weak). Objective 2671 SlimyFeast is in the repo enum. The match between the Gamescom hungry boss, SywoGG's eating boss, and the Slimy Feast room is by theme (A on theme; no direct name statement).
* Other rooms (single-source SywoGG unless marked A with Gamescom): wave-defence room with big mobs whose cast must be interrupted three times for a challenge and a boss who puts a squirrel-head-style debuff on a random player that the healer dispels (S); three-tank "Destruct-O-Derby" room with a central turret that spits a cone of fire giving a moment of opportunity to the tank it hits (not the same tank twice in a row), kill all three tanks within a challenge window of about 3 minutes (S; objectives 2676, 2868, 2869, 2872, 2870 and 2871 in the repo); cubig/jump room with a bird that grants a buff before jumping on purple pads, disorientation for those who skip it (S; Gilded Fowl 2862/4442); Prototentiary (Sneaky Prison): guards with narrow detection cones, spotlights, Warden chase if slow, gate guardian (A: Gamescom, objective 2678); Elemental Hospital ("Master the Elements", objective 2674): beacon carrier attunes beacons so enemies near them are weak, noxious gas outside the bubble (Gamescom, S); HMS Phineas ("Kick Marauders into Deep-Space", 2677): wave defence, "Super Punch" ability from weakening enemies, bonus for hitting asteroids (Gamescom, S); Lost and Found (2673): break crates before the time expires, crates give different effects (Gamescom, S); Protostar petting zoo with Ruffles as a miniboss the tank leads around, dynamite jabbits (Gamescom, S; objective 4561).
No health thresholds are stated for any boss. Timers (3 minutes, 10 seconds) are single-source and approximate.
