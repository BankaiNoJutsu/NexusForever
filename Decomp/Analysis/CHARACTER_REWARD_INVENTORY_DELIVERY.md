## Ownership and persistence

`IInventory.TryReserveSoulboundCreation` composes the existing forced-bound planner with paired Bag slots and Item mutation tokens. It validates the full quantity before reserving anything, freezes template/default charges/sockets, and allocates no GUID or packet during preparation. Existing compatible bound stacks are filled before new private stacks. Public bag/Item references retain their own guards. Unsupported inventory implementations retain their legacy behavior through additive default interface members.

Short synchronous inventory locks order planning and ordinary mutations. Logical save/mutation leases span higher-level work without holding a monitor across an await. Reentrant preparation cannot enter unfinished ordinary creation or the reward notification batch. Normal expiration continues for unrelated items; elapsed time for reserved items is retained and applied after terminal release. Expired existing merges are removed after adoption without erasing the durable claim witness. Tombstone settlement uses the same mutation gate as actor list mutations.

Stage requires an explicit serializable Character transaction, the exact durable source slot and current track cycle/cost, a newly tracked receipt and a valid frozen definition. IDs are allocated only at this stage and retained across verified retry. The receipt contains a versioned immutable payload with every before/after Item snapshot. All leaf writes, claimed state and the receipt must flush and commit together. Invalid or partial enrollment permanently rejects that context.

`CharacterContext.OnSaveSettled` runs after ordinary save-completion owners, even if one observer throws. This lets the aggregate classify the result after every Item/state owner has settled. A failed, unflushed or uncertain unit retains ownership. Dispose does not unlock an ambiguous award. Recovery requires a fresh untracked serializable transaction in the same configured database, validates the full receipt/source plus every Item before changing any leaf state, and permits either exact committed publication or exact absent retry. Conflicting, consumed, moved or otherwise changed rows quarantine the entire operation and require an authoritative reload; a receipt alone cannot resurrect an item.

## Publication and actor lifecycle

Publication preflights all leaves and slots and builds potentially throwing packet payloads before changing live quantities. It adopts every committed baseline and inserts all new stacks before releasing Item or Bag ownership. It then queues the complete packet batch outside locks and refreshes collect objectives once. A new claim cannot start in the middle of that packet batch. Actor invalidation suppresses stale notifications. A send failure cannot undo the committed award or reopen the receipt; it disconnects the actor. Known commits can finish bookkeeping after actor invalidation without packets; uncertain terminal conflicts remain quarantined.

Player direct, deferred, normal and cleanup saves coordinate the inventory lease before Auth work. Busy saves retain their callbacks and requested-save intent; synchronous failures release the lease. Stale quarantined owners do not save old item state during cleanup. Trade, mail, marketplace, item use, decor, vendor, crafting, loot and salvage entry points preflight the logical inventory lease before their spending, removal or delivery effects. This is bounded coordination of current source callers, not a cross-process lock or a blanket transaction around every gameplay action. The future repository still needs database locking, revision checks and retained receipt witnesses.
