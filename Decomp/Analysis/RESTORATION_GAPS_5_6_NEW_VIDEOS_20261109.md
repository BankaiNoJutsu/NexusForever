## Method
- Subtitle search over new videos only: `scripts/gap_newvideos_search.py` and a per-video aggregator `scripts/gap9_agg.py "<fts>" --retail` (one line per video, hits de-duplicated within 20 s; rolling captions otherwise repeat each cue 3 times). Title/description search: `scripts/gap9_newmeta.py "<fts>"` over `meta_fts`, restricted to new videos. Cue windows: `scripts/gap9_cues.py <id> <t0> <t1>`. Contact sheets: `scripts/gap9_sheet.py` (ffmpeg tile). Frames by id: `scripts/gap_frame_by_id.py`.
- OCR sweeps (Windows.Media.Ocr through `ocr_server.ps1`): `scripts/gap9_ocr.py` (frame every N s, whole frame at 1920 plus the top-right 45% x 60% crop at 2.5x) and `scripts/gap9_taxi_sweep.py` (key frames only, whole frame at 1920, pattern list). The sweeps are bounded and sampled; a miss means "not found at that sampling", never "absent".
- Retail rule: only `upload_date` before 20181128. New videos dated 2019 or later, 2020+ (emulator or retrospective) and the undated rows (8) were never counted. Per-year counts of the 1,589 new videos: 2011 to 2012 11; 2013 206; 2014 768; 2015 243; 2016 133; 2017 60; 2018 58; 2019 and later 102; undated 8. So the new material is mostly 2013-2014 (beta and launch era), not the post-launch period the gaps need (2016 to 2018: 251 videos; 50 gameplay-type clips over 4 minutes, 9.5 hours, are dated after the 2016-03-30 launch).

### B.2 The lead: new id 545 `Fast Soulfrost Guide (Wildstar)`, Maximus Grahamicus, 2016-07-18, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CU3_O48cJGE
1280x720, 30 fps, 272 kb/s (low bitrate), 399 s, subtitled. Account is at level 50 with the Arcterra zone map open in a full-screen window; a text overlay appears from about 26 s and grows through 256 s.

**The overlay (read at 26, 130, 166, 196, 236, 256 s; final state at 256 s):**
`Icebound Pouch = 25`, `20 man Boss = 10`, `Pillar = Up To 7 + 8%`, `Live Events = 2 + 4%`, `Challenges = 2 - 5`, `Dailies = 15`, `Weekly = 150`, `Turtle Island = 10 (daily)`, `Frozen Finds = 5`, `5 man Bosses = 5`, `2 Man bosses = 2`.
**What the numbers mean (from the same video's captions):** the whole list is "how much **Soulfrost** you get" per source, with a `+ N%` on the three activities that also advance the zone reward track. Captions: 27 to 32 s "when you fill up this progression track in Arcterra you'll get an icebound pouch, in this icebound pouch you can get 25 Soul Frost"; 123 to 125 s "doing the pillar event will give you 8% towards your track"; 132 to 138 s "the other live events will give you 4% towards your reward track to getting one of those icebound pouches" (and "whack-a-worm or snow-crushed caravan will give you two Soul Frost and 4% towards completing your track"); 321 to 328 s the ice cave "will count as 4% progression towards completing an event" (track); dailies "15 Soul Frost for each of your regular daily quests", the weekly "50 Soul Frost" is captioned as 50 while the overlay reads 150 (an ASR or speech slip; not resolved). **Therefore "Icebound Pouch = 25" is 25 Soulfrost, not 25% of the track and not 25 track points.** The only track increments the guide gives are percentages: pillar 8%, live events 4%, ice cave 4% (and the "Up To 7" is Soulfrost for the pillar). Challenges "2 - 5" (captioned "two two two and then five") are the Soulfrost payouts of the challenge reward track's four milestones; the Challenge Log frame below shows a Pure Soulfrost x2 choice on Reward 1.

**Zone-track counter: hidden in every map frame.** The streamer's facecam covers the top-right 230 px by 172 px of the 1280x720 frame (x 1050 to 1280, y 0 to 172), which is where the right-hand side panel's first box (the position where the zone "Reward Track - Arcterra" panel sits; box spans y 47 to 212) begins; only its lowest 40 px strip is visible. Frames 5, 15, 20, 23, 26, 30, 122, 130, 140, 156, 166, 180, 196, 214, 236, 256 s were read; at 140 s the panel's rounded border and the lower strip of the box are visible under the camera with a mint progress-bar fill starting at the left edge, a crown icon and a gem icon at the right, and **no legible text or counter**. A fill is visible at the left but the camera hides its right end, so no percentage can be read from it. Not found: the panel with its counter. Not usable for the 600,000 versus 6,000,000 question.
**Does "25 = 25% of the track" hold?** No (see above). Does the percentage framing say anything about the scale? Only that retail players saw the panel in percent (as the Lua `progress / cost * 100` expression implies) and that the zone activities were tuned in round percentages (8, 4, 4). Eight percent is 480,000 points on a 6,000,000 track and 48,000 on a 600,000 track; both are round, so **this does not discriminate the two scales.** The 2016-02 PTR deltas between map views (+17,776 and +20,739 on a 600,000 track) were bundles of unseen activity and cannot be compared either.

## Recommendation (conservative)
1. **Taxi (gap 5): no server change.** The new footage adds no short-wallet, interruption or multi-hop witness, so the 2026-11-08 decision stands: one atomic settlement at destination admission, short wallet rejected before departure and again at admission, no early charge, no refund path. The server taxi is still a teleport with no in-flight state. Do not move the debit to purchase or per-leg from the existing single multi-hop clip.
2. **Reward track (gap 6): no server change.** Keep type 4 (zone) and the public 07CE claim disabled. The 6,000,000 versus 600,000 question is still not answerable from video: the only new post-launch zone-track clip hides the counter. Do **not** read "Icebound Pouch = 25" as a track value or a table constant; it is a Soulfrost amount (an item count). The percentage figures (pillar 8%, live events 4%, ice cave 4%) are the first retail-dated statement of how the zone track was fed (2016-07-18, Observed through captions and overlay, commentary-level, not a UI reading); if a future change implements a type 4 producer, these are a lead for per-activity fractions of the track, and the scale question would still need a native read (which expression supplies `nCost`, and does the client scale it).
3. The one table-aligned new fact (challenge track 36 thresholds 300/600/900/1200 shown in a 2016-07-18 Challenge Log) matches what the server already holds; no change.
4. Tooling: before any future corpus-based pass, re-resolve ids by URL (ids were renumbered); the earlier `ocr`/`ocr2` jsonl names are keyed to the old ids.
